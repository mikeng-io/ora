Most agents answer when you type. Ora keeps thinking after you stop.

She lives in our Signal and WhatsApp groups — one workspace, two apps.
When nobody calls her, she holds a position on the room, tracks what's
still open, and decides for herself whether it's worth saying something.

The clip: she raises a reminder on Signal. The plan changes on WhatsApp
instead. She sees her own pending decision — made on the other app — and
drops it. No second raise, no confusion. That's not a chatbox trick;
that's cognition that outlives the model call that produced it.

Built at @AITinkerers Hong Kong's "Agents, Everywhere" hackathon, on top
of prompts and clients we'd already measured — the loop, the deciders, and
the decision log are new today.
